Privacy Policy (English)

Effective (Updated) Date: November 27, 2026
Applicable App: 3D Lotto Simulator / LuckPick (com.xpwmaosldk.lotto)

1. Overview

"3D Lotto Simulator (LuckPick)" respects your privacy and complies with Google Play and Apple App Store Developer Policies.

2. Data Collection and Usage

① Direct Collection: None
The App does not collect or transmit personally identifiable information (PII) to external servers. All settings, draw history, and analytics opt-out preferences are kept solely on your local device.

② Third-Party Ad SDK (Google AdMob)
The App uses Google Mobile Ads SDK to serve ads. Google may collect Advertising ID (AAID/IDFA), coarse device data, and ad interaction logs in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

③ Third-Party Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)
The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase to analyze usage patterns, diagnose stability, and improve features. Collected data includes App Instance ID, screen views, anonymous in-app feature usage events (draws, speed mode, rules/number filters, QR checks, shares, locale changes), device/OS/language/coarse region data, and cookies/session data on the web version. No PII is collected. Learn more at Privacy and Security in Firebase.

3. Opting Out of Analytics & Targeted Advertising

  • Opt Out of Firebase Analytics: Open Settings > Privacy & Usage Analytics in the App and switch to Opt Out (OFF) to immediately stop anonymous usage analytics collection.
  • Opt Out of Personalized Ads: Reset or delete your advertising identifier via Android Settings (Settings > Security & Privacy > Privacy > Ads) or through Google Ads Settings.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under 14 in South Korea), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
